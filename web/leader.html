🌐 Games online

When this Pi is connected to the internet, put the games on your group's own free website (GitHub Pages) so families can play them at home. Pages are hidden from search engines, each session has a hard-to-guess link, and sessions are removed automatically after the number of days you choose. Set-up guide: docs/games-online.md.
